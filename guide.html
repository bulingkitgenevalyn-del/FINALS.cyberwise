📚 Cybersecurity Awareness

Cybersecurity means protecting your devices, accounts and personal information from people who want to steal, damage or misuse them. You do not need to be a computer expert. Good habits stop most attacks.

🔒 Confidentiality

Only the right people can see your information.

✅ Integrity

Your information is not changed by someone else.

🌐 Availability

You can reach your accounts and files when you need them.

🎯 Why are students targeted?

  • Students use many accounts: school, email, social media, games.
  • Many reuse the same password everywhere.
  • Students trust links from friends, free offers and scholarship messages.
  • Hacked social accounts are used to scam your friends and family.

⭐ 5 Golden Habits

  1. Think before you click. Check the sender and the link.
  2. Use strong, different passwords for important accounts.
  3. Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).
  4. Update your phone, apps and computer.
  5. Share less. Keep personal details private.

🔑 Password Security Guide

Test how strong a password is. It is checked only inside your browser. Nothing is sent or saved. Do not type your real password here. Try a similar one instead.

Type something to begin

  • At least 12 characters
  • Has an uppercase letter
  • Has a lowercase letter
  • Has a number
  • Has a symbol (! @ # $ ...)
  • Not a common password or pattern

⚙️ Password Generator

Make a random password or a passphrase.

✅ Password Do's and Don'ts

  • Use a long passphrase of 4 or more random words.
  • Use a different password for each important account.
  • Use a password manager to remember them.
  • Turn on 2FA, especially for email.
  • Do not use your name, birthday or "123456".
  • Do not reuse the same password everywhere.
  • Do not share passwords or OTP codes with anyone.
  • Do not write passwords on sticky notes near your desk.
