ℹ️ About CYBERWISE

Problem: Many students use weak passwords, click suspicious links and share too much online, which puts their accounts and personal information at risk.

Solution: A website that teaches cybersecurity in a simple, interactive way.

Target users: High school and college students.

Technologies: HTML (structure), CSS (design), JavaScript (features), browser localStorage (saved data).
